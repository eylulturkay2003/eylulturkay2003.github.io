CLINICAL RESEARCH TRAINING

Good Clinical Practice ICH-GCP — Full Certificate Course

Formal training in the principles and operational requirements of International Council for Harmonisation Good Clinical Practice (ICH-GCP).

Provider: PV Drug Safety Academy — UdemyCourse length: 2 hours 31 minutes8 sections · 21 lessons
For viewing only — reproduction or redistribution is not permitted.

COURSE OVERVIEW

Good Clinical Practice principles and trial responsibilities.

The course covered the fundamentals and principles of ICH-GCP and the responsibilities of the parties involved in clinical research. Topics included the roles of Institutional Review Boards/Independent Ethics Committees (IRB/IEC), investigators and sponsors; protocol compliance; informed consent; participant safety; and investigational-product management.

TRIAL CONDUCT & OVERSIGHT

Managing conduct, safety and study records.

Training topics included randomisation and unblinding, quality management, safety reporting, sponsor oversight, communication with ethics and regulatory bodies, and handling non-compliance. The course also addressed clinical-trial protocols, the Investigator’s Brochure, data and record management, and essential trial documentation.

TRAINING SCOPE

Formal training, not hands-on trial experience.

This entry records completion of formal ICH-GCP course training. It does not represent hands-on clinical-trial work, investigator or sponsor responsibilities, or independent experience conducting a clinical study.
