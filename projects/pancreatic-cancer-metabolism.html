Selected research / Pancreatic cancer metabolism

BSC DISSERTATION · LITERATURE REVIEW

Pancreatic cancer metabolism

A literature-based discussion of metabolic adaptation and potential vulnerabilities in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).

For viewing only — reproduction or redistribution is not permitted.

OVERVIEW

How metabolic plasticity shapes PDAC biology.

This dissertation synthesizes published research on pancreatic cancer metabolism, with attention to glycolysis and mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) as potential therapeutic vulnerabilities.

RESEARCH QUESTION

What metabolic dependencies could inform therapeutic strategies?

The work considers how PDAC cells may adapt their metabolism and how disrupting mitochondrial Complex I, including discussion of NDUFS1 and CRISPR-Cas9 in the cited literature, has been investigated.

METHODS & FINDINGS

Evidence synthesis, not personal wet-lab experimentation.

The approach is a narrative literature review. The discussion highlights that tumour metabolism is adaptable and that targeting a single pathway may not be sufficient across biological contexts. The dissertation does not report experiments performed by the author to validate these interventions.

LIMITATIONS

Interpret conclusions within the scope of the review.

This is a literature-based dissertation. It does not document a reproducible systematic-search protocol or personal experimental validation; therefore, no claim of screening a specified number of papers is made here.
