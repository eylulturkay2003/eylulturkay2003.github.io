RESEARCH METHODS · AFM / NET4

Basement membrane stiffness and metastasis formation

Type and context. Scientific poster analyzing Reuter and colleagues' published study, Basement membrane stiffness determines metastases formation (Nature Materials, 2021). The poster presents and interprets that study; it is not evidence that I performed AFM measurements, mouse experiments or other wet-lab work.

Research question. How does basement-membrane (BM) mechanical stiffness, including changes associated with loss of Netrin-4 (Net4), relate to tumour-cell dissemination and metastatic formation?

Techniques discussed. The source-study poster describes Net4-deficient mouse models, analysis of laminin/BM organization, atomic force microscopy (AFM) measurements of BM mechanics, and assessment of metastatic outcomes. AFM is a method used in the cited study, not a technique claimed as personally performed by me.

Findings reported by the source study. As summarized in the poster, Net4 loss disrupted laminin architecture, increased BM stiffness and was associated with increased metastatic burden. AFM measurements were presented as evidence that Net4-deficient BMs were stiffer. These are findings attributed to Reuter et al.; they are not original data from this coursework.

My analytical contribution. I analyzed the published work and presented its research question, experimental setup and reported interpretation as a scientific poster, relating BM mechanics to cancer invasion. This describes the poster analysis and communication, not the source-study experiments.

Interpretation and limitations. The poster's summary should be read alongside the full paper for model design, controls, measurement details and statistical analysis. A mouse-model association does not establish that BM stiffness alone determines metastasis in human disease; other matrix, tumour and host factors may contribute. No causal or clinical claim beyond the cited study is made here.

Skills demonstrated. Literature appraisal, distinguishing source-study results from poster authorship, interpreting biomechanical methods, synthesizing a mechanistic story and visual scientific communication.

Primary source: Reuter R, Zendehroud S, Nicolau M, et al. Basement membrane stiffness determines metastases formation. Nature Materials. 2021;20:892–903.
