Selected research / LIMD1–ARIH1

MSC RESEARCH PROJECT · PROTEIN INTERACTION MAPPING

LIMD1–ARIH1 interaction mapping

Mapping LIMD1 regions involved in association with ARIH1 using truncation constructs and biochemical pulldown assays.

Molecular pathologyExperimental cancer biologyProject overview

RESEARCH QUESTION

Which LIMD1 regions contribute to its association with ARIH1?

The project used a panel of LIMD1 truncation constructs to investigate protein association. Recombinant protein expression, affinity purification and HEK293FT pulldown assays formed the experimental workflow.

APPROACH

Construct design through pulldown analysis.

  • 18 LIMD1 constructs
  • Recombinant expression
  • Affinity purification
  • HEK293FT pulldown
  • Sequence confirmation

A FLAG-ARIH1 construct was generated and sequence-confirmed. LIMD1 truncation constructs were then used in a biochemical interaction-mapping workflow to compare association across regions.

EVIDENCE & INTERPRETATION

What the experimental record supports.

The work establishes a systematic construct panel and a sequence-confirmed ARIH1 reagent for probing LIMD1 association. Interpretation of individual interaction regions depends on assay controls and comparable bait recovery across conditions.

LIMITATIONS

Controls and recovery matter.

Missing controls and variable bait recovery were identified as limitations. These factors constrain how confidently differences between constructs can be attributed to specific binding regions; conclusions should be interpreted in that context.

This page summarizes the project methods and evaluation available in the portfolio. It does not claim a definitive interaction domain or quantitative result.
