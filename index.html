About

Following the biology from molecular mechanism to disease context.

My training spans experimental cancer biology, protein interaction studies, pancreatic cancer metabolism, RNA-seq analysis and diagnostic histopathology workflows.

I am interested in the mechanisms that shape tumour progression, treatment resistance and disease heterogeneity, and in how experimental evidence can be interpreted with care.

This portfolio brings together selected projects, methods and evidence, including the limitations that inform how results should be read.
