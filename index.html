MSc Research Project

LIMD1–ARIH1 interaction mapping

Mapping regions of LIMD1 involved in association with ARIH1 using truncation constructs, recombinant protein expression, affinity purification and HEK293FT pulldown assays.

  • 18 LIMD1 constructs investigated
  • FLAG-ARIH1 construct generated and sequence-confirmed
  • Biochemical interaction mapping
  • Critical evaluation of missing controls and variable bait recovery
